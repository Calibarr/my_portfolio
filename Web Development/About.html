About our Business and it's History

About 10 years ago, we first set up a salon named Cal's Curls in Cork City. For 5 years we continued to expand our business, creating extra locations in Dublin and Clare.

However, in 2020 when the pandemic began, it became clear that the everyday salon experience was no longer going to cut it.Due to nationwide quarantine, business was larger then ever. Yet there was no way to offer our services.

This is why over the next 6 months, we planned, organized, advertised and began our mobile haircut service, rebranding to our new name, Ride or Dye.

We began with a small crew operating primarily around Cork. Our staff were commited to helping people regain a sense of normalcy and self care. Having sponsored local influencers, the initial demand became much larger then was anticipated, causing immediate need for expansion. The risk had paid off, and over the next few months we began establishing networks of hair stylists around the country.

The World of Mobile Hairdressing

It was at this point that we realized how difficult the usual salon experience had been for so many.

In rural areas, salon options had always been limited. For those who had difficulty leaving home, be it through age, physical disability or mental health issues, a salon was a very difficult experience.

Getting the opportunity to provide the care these people deserved was incredibly fulfilling for our staff and helped those affected reconnect with the world in a way they couldn't before.

That's why, when quarantines were beginning to lift and salons were reopening, our staff all agreed that the work they did was too important to quit and decided to move ahead with Ride or Dye.
